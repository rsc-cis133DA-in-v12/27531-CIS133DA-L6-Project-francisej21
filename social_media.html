Social Media

Social media refers to websites and applications that allow people to create, share, and interact with content online. People can use social media to communicate, share photos and videos, follow organizations, and discover information. Businesses can also use social media to increase awareness, connect with customers, and promote their products and services.

Social Media Comparison

Facebook

Facebook is a social networking platform that allows individuals and businesses to share posts, photos, videos, events, and other information. Businesses can use Facebook to build awareness, communicate with customers, advertise, and create communities.

Source: facebook.png

Instagram

Instagram is a social media platform focused on photos, videos, stories, and short-form video content. Businesses can use Instagram to showcase products, develop their brand, communicate with followers, and reach new audiences through visual content.

Source: instagram.svg

LinkedIn

LinkedIn is a professional networking platform used for career development, recruiting, professional connections, and business communication. Companies can use LinkedIn to establish credibility, share business updates, recruit employees, and develop professional partnerships.
